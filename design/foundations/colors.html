Rules. Text pairs must clear 4.5:1 (WCAG AA, 16sp body). Interactive borders and state indicators must clear 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11) — that is why --outline exists separately from --outline-variant, which is decorative only and has no minimum. The iMessage/SMS fills are semantics, not theme, and never change with dynamic colour — but they are still contrast-constrained: the SMS green keeps its recognisable hue and darkens its text instead, because white on #34C759 is only 2.22:1. On a real device, dynamic colour (wallpaper) replaces the neutral and primary roles, so the outline and surface ratios above hold for the static palette only.
